# What's new

New features show up here when they land, and the app shows each one once in its What's new window. A feature marked `toggle: off` stays off until you turn it on there or in Settings.

The changelog lists every change; this file lists only what someone would want to try. Each feature is a `##` heading, a list of `id`, `since` and `toggle`, then a few lines on what it does.

## Search by meaning

- id: meaning
- since: 2026-11-25
- toggle: off
- needs: typesafe-key

Find messages by what they mean, not only by their words: "when do we ship" finds "release goes out friday". It runs on your own TypeSafe key, which stays on this computer: your search and the channel's newest messages go from here straight to TypeSafe's Jev under your account, never through the Channels relay. Pick Search by meaning at the top of the search results, or press ⌘ Enter.
