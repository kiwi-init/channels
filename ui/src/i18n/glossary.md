## Tone

- **pt-BR:** "você", plain and informal, as short as the English.
- **ja:** です・ます for sentences; plain nouns for buttons and labels.
- **ko:** 합니다체 for sentences; short nouns for buttons and labels.
- **zh-CN:** short, with full-width punctuation (，。：（）).
